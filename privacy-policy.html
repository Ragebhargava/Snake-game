Snake (Retro LCD) ("the App") is a single-player game. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or network requests of any kind while you play.

The App saves your high score locally on your device only, using standard browser storage (localStorage). This data:

  • is not shared with the developer or any third party,

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
